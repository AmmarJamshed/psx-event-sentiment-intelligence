4. The relationship in plain words

Money stress hurts most FX crises, IMF stress, rate shocks, and inflation/energy pain create the largest typical weekly swings.
Security scares hit fast India tensions and terror attacks often create sharp risk-off weeks (sometimes the single worst week).
Politics matters, but less on average Elections and leadership fights move stocks, yet the average weekly shake is smaller than economic stress.

Simple rule: Event type + how bad it is → stock move over the next week, after checking the next trading day (weekends/holidays). Correlation is not the same as causation.

7. What could change the path next?

Could push PSX UP

  • Smooth IMF reviews — unlocks money + calms the rupee
  • Stable / stronger PKR
  • Real Gulf FDI / deposits (Saudi/UAE style inflows)
  • Soft inflation + careful rate cuts
  • Export / remittance strength
  • Quiet geopolitics

Could push PSX DOWN

  • PKR crash / reserve stress
  • IMF programme slip
  • Energy tariff / circular-debt flare
  • Political rupture
  • India / security escalation
  • Oil spike + global risk-off
